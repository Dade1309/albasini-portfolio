01 / About
The journey so far
Travel changed the way I see the world.

I’m Davide Albasini, an Italian traveler and photographer currently based between Italy and the United States, where I have spent the past four years.

Living away from home changed the way I see the world. It gave me the opportunity to discover new places, experience different environments, and gradually realize how important travel was becoming in my life.

My passion for photography began naturally during my first trips. Wherever I went, I felt the need to document everything—the landscapes, the streets, the light, the people, and the small moments in between. I wanted to remember not only where I had been, but also how those places made me feel.

At first, photography was simply a way to preserve memories. Then I bought my first camera, and everything changed.

I started paying closer attention to composition, light, atmosphere, and the details that make every place unique. Photography became more than documentation. It became a way of experiencing travel differently and telling stories through what I saw.

Today, I know what I want to build my life around: traveling, discovering the world, and documenting the journey along the way.

This website marks the beginning of that story—a collection of the places I’ve experienced, the moments I’ve captured, and the journeys still ahead.

I want to make travel my life—and document every part of the journey.
